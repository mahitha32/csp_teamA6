# Smart Village Governance Platform — System Manuals

## 1. User (Villager) Manual

### Getting Started
1. **Download the App**: Install the Gadepudi CSP app on your Android device.
2. **Registration**: Open the app, enter your mobile number, and complete the OTP verification. Set up your profile.

### Filing a Complaint
1. Tap the **"+"** button or **"New Complaint"** on the home screen.
2. Select the category (e.g., Water Supply, Roads).
3. Take a photo of the issue directly from the app.
4. Allow location access to pin the exact GPS coordinates.
5. Tap **Submit**. You will receive a Complaint ID (e.g., `CMP2026015`).

### Tracking & Feedback
- Navigate to the **"My Complaints"** tab to see the live status.
- Once a worker resolves the issue, you will receive a push notification.
- Please rate the service out of 5 stars and leave remarks to help us improve.

---

## 2. Admin Manual

### Dashboard Overview
- Navigate to `http://localhost:3000/dashboard` and log in with admin credentials.
- The **Dashboard** provides a bird's-eye view of all complaints, worker performance, and resolution rates.

### Managing Complaints
- Go to the **"Complaints"** tab.
- Click the **"Assign Worker"** icon (clipboard) on any "Under Review" complaint.
- Select an appropriate worker based on the department (e.g., assign an electrician for streetlights).
- The worker will instantly receive a notification on their mobile app.

### Analytics and Insights
- The **"Reports"** and **"Survey Insights"** tabs show long-term trends and pre-loaded field survey data. Use these for presentations or official government reports.

---

## 3. Worker Manual

### Receiving Tasks
- Log in to the worker app using your registered mobile number.
- Navigate to the **"My Assignments"** tab to see pending tasks.

### Resolving Issues
1. Tap on an assignment to view the exact GPS location and user-uploaded photo.
2. Navigate to the location and perform the required maintenance.
3. Once completed, tap **"Update Status"**.
4. Select **"Resolved"**, upload a photo of the completed work, and add any resolution notes.
5. The admin and the villager will be notified automatically.
