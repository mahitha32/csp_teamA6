# Faculty Demonstration Flow (10 Minutes)

This script is designed for a seamless, high-impact presentation during project exhibitions or viva.

## Step 1: Context & Problem Statement (1 min)
**Talking Point**: "Good morning. Our Community Service Project (CSP) focused on Gadepudi Village. During our field survey, we found that 34% of villagers faced critical road issues and 26% faced water supply problems. The manual complaint system was slow, lacked transparency, and had no accountability. We built the Smart Village Governance Platform to digitize this entire workflow."

## Step 2: The Villager Experience - Mobile App (2 mins)
**Action**: Mirror your Android phone or use an emulator showing the Flutter app.
- Show the login/registration screen.
- **Demonstrate**: Submit a new complaint. Take a live photo of an object, select "Water Supply", and submit.
- **Highlight**: GPS coordinates are fetched automatically. The UI is clean, accessible, and in regional languages (if implemented).

## Step 3: The Admin Experience - React Dashboard (3 mins)
**Action**: Switch to the laptop showing the React Admin Dashboard.
- **Demonstrate**: Show the real-time update of the complaint just submitted.
- Navigate to the **Dashboard** to show KPIs, resolution rates, and charts.
- **Highlight**: The Trust Blue government-style theme, the automated charting, and the data scale (mention the 1000 realistically generated demo records).
- **Action**: Assign the newly submitted complaint to a worker.

## Step 4: The Worker Experience (1 min)
**Action**: Switch back to the mobile app (logged in as Worker).
- **Demonstrate**: Receive the notification. Accept the task. Mark it as "Resolved" and upload a "fixed" photo.

## Step 5: Transparency & Impact (2 mins)
**Action**: Show the Admin Dashboard again.
- Navigate to the **Community Impact** page.
- **Highlight**: "Before vs After" metrics. Emphasize the Development Score Index.
- Show the **Timeline Visualization** of the complaint to prove accountability.

## Step 6: Conclusion (1 min)
**Talking Point**: "This platform is fully scalable, cloud-ready, and aligns with Digital India initiatives. It transforms a reactive panchayat into a proactive, data-driven local government."
